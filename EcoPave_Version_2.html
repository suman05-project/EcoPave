Material library

Give waste a second purpose.

Learn the potential role of different waste streams in paving products. Actual use depends on material quality, processing and testing.

🧱

Recycled concrete (RCA)

Processed concrete from demolition can partially replace natural coarse or fine aggregate in cement-bound pavers.

Aggregate replacement
🏺

Brick and tile waste

Crushed, graded brick or tile can be investigated as an alternative aggregate, subject to absorption and strength checks.

Alternative aggregate
🔷

Waste glass

Washed and size-controlled glass may be used as fine aggregate or finely ground supplementary material. ASR risk must be assessed.

Fine aggregate / powder
🌫️

Fly ash

Suitable fly ash can act as a supplementary cementitious material in cement-based mixes when its properties and proportions are verified.

Cementitious addition
♻️

Waste plastic

Selected thermoplastics have been studied in plastic-sand composite pavers. Sorting, controlled processing and emissions safety are essential.

Polymer composite
⚫

Waste rubber

Crumb rubber can be explored as a partial modifier or aggregate replacement; it may affect stiffness, strength and abrasion performance.

Rubber-modified mix
◼

Ceramic waste

Crushed ceramic sanitaryware or tiles can be assessed as aggregate replacement after cleaning, grading and absorption testing.

Alternative aggregate

About the project

Engineering a more circular future.

EcoPave is an educational decision-support prototype that connects waste-material options with possible paving applications and manufacturing knowledge.

01 · ReduceExplore ways to reduce dependence on virgin construction materials.
02 · ReuseIdentify waste streams that may be processed into useful paving resources.
03 · VerifyEncourage testing for strength, absorption, abrasion and durability before use.
Engineering disclaimer: Recommendations on this website are conceptual and educational. They are not approved mix proportions, structural design advice or a guarantee of compliance. Do not use a suggested mix for construction without material characterization, trial batches, laboratory testing and review against applicable standards (including the current edition of IS 15658 and relevant material standards). Do not heat or melt waste plastic in an uncontrolled setting; plastic processing requires suitable industrial controls and emissions management.

Learning references: BIS IS 15658 (Precast concrete blocks for paving); research on recycled C&D aggregates in paver blocks; Concrete Society guidance on recycled glass aggregate and alkali-silica reaction.
